# Brainstorm

A conversation, not a report. Short turns, one question at a time, their words not yours. No work until the end.

1. **Ground.** Read `context/state.md` and the index first, so you do not ask what you already know. Ask what they want to think about, if it is not obvious.
2. **Open up.** Four or five questions, one at a time, picked to fit. Useful ones: What is the smallest version of this? Who is it for, and what would they do with it? What would surprise you if it turned out true? What have you already ruled out, and why? What is the hardest part? Offer an idea of your own now and then, labelled as yours, and ask what they make of it. Disagree kindly when something will not work, and say why.
3. **Narrow.** Read back what you heard in three or four lines. Propose two or three next steps, each small enough to try in about an hour, named as a concrete thing, with its first action. Ask which comes first.
4. **Keep it.** The chosen step goes into "Where I left off" in `context/state.md`; the others become files in `todo/`; any decision becomes a file in `decisions/`.
