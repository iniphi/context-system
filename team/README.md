# team

The routines for a small team, two to five people, sharing one store.

To be written here, as prompts any agent can follow (DESIGN.md, sections 2 and 5):

- `start.md`: open a session; read the shared state, your own state, and anything handed to you.
- `close.md`: end a session; rewrite only your own state and the parts of the shared one you changed.
- `handoff.md`: hand work to a teammate. A handoff is addressed to a person, never left as a loose to-do.

What a team adds to the loop: a state per person beside the shared one, names on records (who owns, who wrote, who decided), claims on work in progress, and a line between private notes and the shared store.

Stores that suit a team: GitHub, Notion, Google Drive.
