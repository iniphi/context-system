# team

The routines for a small team, two to five people, sharing one store.

Written:

- `start.md`: open a session. Who is working, then their hand-offs and to-dos first, their own state, and the shared state.
- `close.md`: end a session. Rewrite only your own state and the parts of the shared one you changed; anything that did not close goes to its owner's to-dos.
- `todos.md`: per-person to-dos kept in the team's repo, the hand-off mechanism, the bar for writing one, and how to seed the lists before an event.

What a team adds to the loop: a state per person beside the shared one, names on records (who owns, who wrote, who decided), hand-offs written into the person's own to-do list rather than left as a loose note, claims on work in progress, and a line between private notes and the shared store.

Learned at a hackathon (GLAMhack 2026): an idea travels with the person who had it at no cost; what gets lost is the hand-off to someone else. People do not check a board they were never introduced to, so the list is introduced through `start` and `close` instead of being a place people are told to look.

Stores that suit a team: GitHub (the routines are written for it), Notion, Google Drive.
